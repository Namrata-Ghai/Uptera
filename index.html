The Problem We Are Solving

Delhi-NCR has a logistics gap, not a generosity gap.

Every day, thousands of wearable jackets, school textbooks, and household goods get tossed into mixed garbage or sit unused in cupboards because residents don't know which local NGO actually needs them—or lack time to drop them off.

Once mixed into municipal waste streams, clean cotton and paper are contaminated forever, adding to Delhi’s towering landfills while grassroots shelters struggle to afford basic supplies.

Our Purpose

Why Uptera Exists

Traditional charity drives often fail because NGOs receive bags of torn, unusable items they must pay to dispose of, while donors never know if their items actually helped anyone.

Uptera fixes both sides with simple, transparent technology.

01

Dignity-First Donations

Our Vision AI screens photos before pickup so partner NGOs only receive clean, intact, weather-appropriate clothes and complete books—never discarded junk.

02

Hyperlocal Route Clustering

Instead of single-item trips across NCR traffic, Uptera batches pickups by colony and RWA—cutting transport costs and carbon emissions per kilogram diverted.

03

End-to-End Accountability

Donors receive clear confirmation when their items reach the matched NGO, turning one-time decluttering into a measurable neighborhood habit.
